# Overview

Interleukin-3 (IL-3) is a pleiotropic hematopoietic cytokine that supports the survival, proliferation, and differentiation of multipotent hematopoietic progenitor cells. Unlike lineage-specific cytokines, IL-3 acts on a broad range of early blood cell precursors, making it an important component of hematopoietic stem cell culture and immune cell differentiation protocols.

IL-3 is widely used in hematopoietic stem cell research, dendritic cell generation, mast cell culture, myeloid differentiation, and cytokine-based expansion systems.

---

# Biological Function

IL-3 regulates several biological processes, including:

* Promotes hematopoietic progenitor cell survival
* Stimulates proliferation of early blood cell precursors
* Supports myeloid lineage differentiation
* Enhances mast cell growth
* Promotes basophil development
* Supports dendritic cell generation
* Reduces apoptosis of hematopoietic cells

---

# Mechanism of Action

IL-3 binds to the **IL-3 receptor**, composed of:

* **IL-3Rα (CD123)**
* **Common β chain (βc/CD131)**

Receptor activation induces intracellular signaling through:

* JAK2-STAT5 pathway
* PI3K-AKT pathway
* MAPK/ERK pathway

These signaling pathways regulate cell survival, proliferation, differentiation, and hematopoietic lineage commitment.

IL-3 is widely used for:

# Advantages

* Supports multiple hematopoietic lineages
* Promotes progenitor cell survival
* Enhances expansion of early hematopoietic cells
* Widely used in hematopoietic differentiation protocols
* Well-characterized cytokine signaling
* Compatible with serum-free hematopoietic media

---

# Limitations

* Activity depends on IL-3 receptor expression
* Limited effects on mature blood cells
* Rarely used as a single cytokine
* Sensitive to repeated freeze-thaw cycles
* May alter lineage commitment depending on cytokine combinations

# Key Takeaways

* IL-3 is a multipotent hematopoietic cytokine that promotes survival and proliferation of early blood cell progenitors.
* It signals through the **IL-3 receptor (CD123/CD131)**, activating JAK2-STAT5, PI3K-AKT, and MAPK/ERK pathways.
* IL-3 is widely used for hematopoietic stem cell culture, myeloid differentiation, dendritic cell generation, and mast cell culture.
* It is commonly combined with **SCF, TPO, FLT3L, and IL-6** in hematopoietic expansion media.
* Typical working concentrations range from **1-20 ng/mL**, depending on the application.
