# Overview

Fms-like Tyrosine Kinase 3 Ligand (Flt3 Ligand or FLT3L) is a hematopoietic cytokine that promotes the survival, proliferation, and differentiation of early hematopoietic progenitor cells. It plays a central role in dendritic cell development and is widely used in hematopoietic stem cell expansion and immune cell differentiation protocols.

Unlike lineage-specific cytokines such as M-CSF or G-CSF, Flt3 Ligand acts primarily on early progenitor populations, maintaining multipotency and supporting the development of multiple hematopoietic lineages, particularly dendritic cells.

---

# Biological Function

Flt3 Ligand regulates several key processes in hematopoiesis, including:

* Promotes hematopoietic progenitor survival
* Supports stem and progenitor cell expansion
* Stimulates dendritic cell development
* Enhances lymphoid progenitor proliferation
* Maintains early hematopoietic precursors
* Supports immune system development
* Promotes hematopoietic recovery following myelosuppression

---

# Mechanism of Action

Flt3 Ligand binds to the **Flt3 receptor (CD135)** expressed on hematopoietic stem cells and early progenitor cells.

Ligand binding induces receptor dimerization and activation of downstream signaling pathways, including:

* PI3K-AKT pathway
* MAPK/ERK pathway
* JAK-STAT pathway
* SRC family kinase pathways

These pathways regulate stem cell survival, proliferation, differentiation, and hematopoietic lineage commitment.

# Common Applications

Flt3 Ligand is widely used for:

* Hematopoietic stem cell culture
* Dendritic cell differentiation
* Expansion of hematopoietic progenitors
* Bone marrow cell culture
* Immune cell development studies
* Cancer immunology research
* Vaccine development
* Hematopoietic regeneration studies

# Advantages

* Supports early hematopoietic progenitors
* Essential for dendritic cell generation
* Enhances stem cell expansion
* Well-characterized signaling pathway
* Frequently used in immunology and stem cell research
* Compatible with serum-free and defined media

---

# Limitations

* Activity depends on Flt3 receptor expression
* Does not support mature immune cell proliferation
* Sensitive to repeated freeze-thaw cycles
* Usually requires combination with other cytokines
* Differentiation outcomes depend on cytokine combinations

# Key Takeaways

* Flt3 Ligand (FLT3L) is a key cytokine regulating **early hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells**.
* It signals through the **Flt3 receptor (CD135)** to activate PI3K-AKT, MAPK/ERK, JAK-STAT, and SRC signaling pathways.
* Flt3 Ligand is widely used for **hematopoietic stem cell expansion, dendritic cell differentiation, and immune cell development**.
* It is commonly combined with **SCF, TPO, IL-3, IL-6, GM-CSF, and G-CSF** in hematopoietic culture systems.
* Typical working concentrations range from **10-100 ng/mL**, with **50-100 ng/mL** commonly used for stem cell expansion and dendritic cell generation.
