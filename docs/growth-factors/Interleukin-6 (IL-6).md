# Overview

Interleukin-6 (IL-6) is a pleiotropic cytokine involved in immune regulation, inflammation, hematopoiesis, and tissue regeneration. It influences the survival, proliferation, and differentiation of numerous cell types and is widely used in mammalian cell culture, particularly for hematopoietic, immune, hybridoma, and stem cell applications.

In vitro, IL-6 is commonly incorporated into cytokine cocktails for hematopoietic stem cell expansion, B-cell differentiation, hybridoma generation, and maintenance of selected pluripotent stem cell cultures.

---

# Biological Function

IL-6 regulates numerous biological processes, including:

* Promotes cell survival
* Supports hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells
* Stimulates B-cell maturation
* Enhances plasma cell differentiation
* Regulates immune responses
* Supports tissue regeneration
* Modulates inflammatory signaling

---

# Mechanism of Action

IL-6 binds to the **IL-6 receptor (IL-6R/CD126)**, which associates with the signal-transducing receptor **gp130 (CD130)**.

Activation of the receptor complex stimulates multiple intracellular signaling pathways, including:

* JAK-STAT3 pathway
* PI3K-AKT pathway
* MAPK/ERK pathway

These pathways regulate cell proliferation, differentiation, survival, and inflammatory responses.

# Advantages

* Supports hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells
* Enhances hybridoma growth and antibody production
* Promotes B-cell maturation
* Widely used in immunology research
* Well-characterized cytokine signaling pathway
* Compatible with serum-free hematopoietic media

---

# Limitations

* Activity depends on IL-6 receptor expression
* Broad biological activity may influence multiple cell populations
* Rarely used alone in culture
* Sensitive to repeated freeze-thaw cycles
* High concentrations may alter differentiation outcomes

# Key Takeaways

* IL-6 is a multifunctional cytokine involved in immune regulation, hematopoiesis, and tissue repair.
* It signals through the **IL-6R/gp130 receptor complex**, activating JAK-STAT3, PI3K-AKT, and MAPK/ERK signaling pathways.
* IL-6 is widely used for hematopoietic stem cell culture, hybridoma maintenance, B-cell differentiation, and immune cell research.
* It is commonly combined with **SCF, TPO, IL-3, and FLT3L** in hematopoietic expansion protocols.
* Typical working concentrations range from **1-100 ng/mL**, depending on the application.
