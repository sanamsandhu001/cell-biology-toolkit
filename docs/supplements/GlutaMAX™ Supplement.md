# Overview

GlutaMAX™ Supplement is a stabilized dipeptide form of L-glutamine designed to provide a consistent source of glutamine while overcoming the instability of free L-glutamine in aqueous solutions. Unlike free glutamine, GlutaMAX gradually releases glutamine through enzymatic cleavage, reducing ammonia accumulation and improving medium stability.

It has become the preferred glutamine source for many mammalian cell culture applications, particularly long-term cultures and metabolically active cell types.

# Biological Role

GlutaMAX™ supports cell culture by:

* Providing a stable source of glutamine
* Supporting cellular energy metabolism
* Promoting protein and nucleotide synthesis
* Reducing ammonia accumulation
* Improving cell viability
* Increasing media stability during long-term culture

# Mechanism of Action

L-Alanyl-L-Glutamine

↓

Enzymatic Cleavage

↓

L-Glutamine + Alanine

↓

Sustained Cellular Metabolism

This gradual release minimizes spontaneous glutamine degradation and reduces toxic ammonia formation.

# Advantages

* Highly stable glutamine source
* Reduced ammonia production
* Longer media shelf life
* Improved experimental reproducibility
* Better cell viability during long-term culture
* Compatible with most mammalian culture systems

# Limitations

* More expensive than free L-glutamine
* Some legacy protocols specifically require free L-glutamine
* Should not be added if already present in the basal medium
