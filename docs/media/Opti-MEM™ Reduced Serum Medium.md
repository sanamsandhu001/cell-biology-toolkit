| Storage             | 2-8°C, protected from light                                      |

## Overview

Opti-MEM™ Reduced Serum Medium is a modified MEM formulation designed to maximize transfection efficiency while maintaining cell viability under reduced-serum conditions. Its proprietary nutrient composition minimizes the adverse effects of serum deprivation and improves nucleic acid delivery using lipid-based and other chemical transfection reagents.

Unlike conventional growth media, Opti-MEM is intended primarily for short-term transfection workflows rather than routine long-term cell culture.

* Standard medium for Lipofectamine-based protocols

# Best Practices

* Use healthy, actively dividing cells for optimal transfection efficiency.
* Prepare nucleic acid-transfection reagent complexes according to the manufacturer's instructions.
* Use high-quality, endotoxin-free plasmid DNA or RNA.
* Optimize reagent-to-nucleic acid ratios for each cell type.
* Replace Opti-MEM with the routine growth medium after the recommended transfection period unless otherwise specified.
