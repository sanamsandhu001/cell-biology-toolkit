# Ham's F-12 Nutrient Mixture

## Quick Facts

| Feature            | Information                                |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| Medium Type        | Nutrient-rich basal medium                 |
| Original Developer | Richard G. Ham                             |
| Best For           | Epithelial cells and serum-reduced culture |
| Parent Medium      | DMEM/F-12                                  |
| Common Supplements | FBS, ITS, GlutaMAX/L-Glutamine             |
| Storage            | 2-8°C, protected from light                |

## Overview

Ham's F-12 Nutrient Mixture (F-12) is a nutrient-rich basal medium developed to support mammalian cells under low-serum and serum-free conditions. Compared with earlier media such as MEM, it contains a broader range of amino acids, vitamins, trace elements, and specialized nutrients, making it particularly suitable for epithelial cells, endocrine cells, CHO cells, and chemically defined culture systems.

F-12 is also one of the two parent media used to formulate DMEM/F-12, one of the most widely used basal media for stem cell and neural cell culture.

# Key Features

* Rich in vitamins and trace elements
* Designed for low-serum and serum-free culture
* Supports epithelial and endocrine cell growth
* Suitable for chemically defined media
* Frequently used in biotechnology applications
* Foundation of DMEM/F-12 formulations

* Endocrine cell lines

* Recombinant protein production
* Biopharmaceutical development
* Serum-free culture systems
* Chemically defined media

### Stem Cell Research

* Component of DMEM/F-12
* Neural stem cell culture (via DMEM/F-12)
* Organoid media formulations

---

# Advantages

* Rich micronutrient composition
* Excellent for serum-reduced culture
* Supports epithelial cell growth
* Compatible with chemically defined media
* Widely used in biotechnology
* Forms the basis of DMEM/F-12

---

# Limitations

* Less commonly used alone than DMEM or DMEM/F-12
* Requires supplementation
* Not ideal for most neuronal cultures
* Less suitable for rapidly proliferating immortalized cell lines than DMEM

---

# Comparison with Related Media

| Medium     | Best Suited For                                                |
| :--------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ham's F-12 | Epithelial cells, CHO cells and serum-free culture             |
| DMEM       | Rapidly proliferating adherent cell lines                      |
| DMEM/F-12  | Stem cells, neural cells and primary cultures                  |
| MEM        | Routine adherent cell culture with lower nutrient requirements |
