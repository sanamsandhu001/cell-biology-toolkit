# DMEM/F-12 (Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium/Nutrient Mixture F-12)

## Quick Facts

| Feature | Information |
|----------|-------------|
| Full Name | Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium/Nutrient Mixture F-12 |
| Medium Type | Basal Medium |
| Developed From | DMEM + Ham's F-12 (1:1 mixture) |
| Common Formats | With or without L-Glutamine, HEPES, and Phenol Red |
| Serum Requirement | Usually supplemented with serum or defined additives |
| Typical Applications | Stem cells, neural cells, primary cells, organoids |
| Storage | 2-8°C, protected from light |

---

# Overview

DMEM/F-12 is a versatile basal medium created by combining Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium (DMEM) and Ham's F-12 Nutrient Mixture in a 1:1 ratio. It combines the high nutrient content of DMEM with the extensive micronutrients, vitamins, amino acids, and trace elements present in F-12, making it suitable for a wide variety of mammalian cell types.

Because of its balanced composition, DMEM/F-12 is extensively used for serum-free and chemically defined culture systems, particularly in stem cell biology, neuroscience, epithelial cell culture, and organoid research.

# Key Features

- Combines the advantages of DMEM and Ham's F-12.
- Rich in vitamins, amino acids, and trace elements.
- Supports serum-free and chemically defined culture.
- Suitable for both established cell lines and primary cells.
- Frequently used as the basal medium for stem cell and organoid protocols.
- Available in multiple formulations to suit different applications.

# Typical Applications

### Stem Cell Culture

- Human embryonic stem cells (hESCs)
- Human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs)
- Neural stem cells (NSCs)
- Neural progenitor cells (NPCs)

### Neural Cell Culture

- Primary neurons
- Astrocytes
- Microglia
- Oligodendrocytes

### Primary Cell Culture

- Fibroblasts
- Endothelial cells
- Selected hepatocyte protocols
- Various epithelial cells

### Organoid Systems

- Brain organoids
- Cortical organoids
- Retinal organoids
- Intestinal organoids

# Advantages

- Highly versatile basal medium
- Rich micronutrient composition
- Supports serum-free culture
- Compatible with chemically defined media
- Excellent for stem cell and neural applications
- Widely validated across diverse mammalian cell types

---

# Limitations

- Requires supplementation for most applications
- More expensive than standard DMEM
- Multiple commercial formulations may differ
- Not optimal for every immortalized cell line
