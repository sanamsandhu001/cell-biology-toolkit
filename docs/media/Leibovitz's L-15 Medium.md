## Overview

Leibovitz's L-15 Medium (L-15) is a specialized basal medium designed for mammalian cell culture under atmospheric air without the need for a CO₂ incubator. Instead of relying on a bicarbonate-CO₂ buffering system, L-15 uses phosphate buffers and free-base amino acids to maintain physiological pH.

Its CO₂-independent formulation makes it the preferred medium for live-cell microscopy, cell transport, tissue dissection, and field-based experiments where conventional incubator conditions are unavailable.

---

# Key Features

* CO₂-independent buffering system
* Stable physiological pH under atmospheric air
* Suitable for prolonged live-cell imaging
* Ideal for transport of viable cells and tissues
* Supports selected mammalian, fish, and amphibian cell cultures
* Useful for short-term manipulation outside incubators

# Advantages

* Does not require a CO₂ incubator
* Excellent pH stability in atmospheric air
* Ideal for microscopy and imaging experiments
* Suitable for transporting living cells and tissues
* Useful for field and mobile laboratory applications
* Supports a variety of specialized culture systems

---

# Limitations

* Not intended for routine culture in 5% CO₂ incubators
* Requires supplementation
* Not suitable for most stem cell cultures
* Less commonly used than DMEM or RPMI for routine mammalian cell culture

# Best Practices

* Use L-15 for experiments performed outside a CO₂ incubator.
* Equilibrate medium to the working temperature before use.
* Supplement according to the requirements of the target cell type.
* Avoid prolonged culture in bicarbonate-based incubator conditions unless specifically validated.
* Follow cell bank recommendations for long-term maintenance media.
