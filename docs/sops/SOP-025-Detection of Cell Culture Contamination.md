# SOP-025: Detection of Cell Culture Contamination

## Purpose

To provide a standardized procedure for the routine detection, documentation, and reporting of contamination in mammalian cell cultures.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to the routine examination of adherent and suspension mammalian cell cultures for bacterial, fungal, yeast, and suspected mycoplasma contamination.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Inspecting cultures before every manipulation.
* Recognizing signs of contamination.
* Isolating suspected contaminated cultures.
* Recording observations and notifying the laboratory supervisor.

---

## Required Materials

### Equipment

* Inverted microscope
* Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Cell culture vessels
* Laboratory notebook or electronic record system
* Personal protective equipment (PPE)

---

## Procedure

### 1. Inspect the Culture Vessel

Before opening the culture vessel, examine:

* Medium clarity
* Medium color
* Presence of floating particles
* Integrity of the culture vessel

---

### 2. Microscopic Examination

Using an inverted microscope, assess the culture for:

* Cell morphology
* Cell attachment
* Cellular debris
* Unusual particulate movement
* Fungal hyphae or yeast-like structures
* Abnormal cell behavior

---

### 3. Assess the Culture

Compare observations with the expected appearance of the cell line.

If contamination is suspected:

* Do not use the culture for experiments.
* Clearly label the culture as **Suspected Contamination**.
* Separate it from uncontaminated cultures pending further investigation.

---

### 4. Document Findings

Record:

* Type of abnormality observed
* Culture identification
* Date and time
* Operator initials

---

### 5. Report the Incident

Report suspected contamination according to laboratory procedures.

Proceed with confirmatory testing when required.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter               | Acceptance Criterion                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Medium appearance       | Clear and free of visible particles |
| Cell morphology         | Typical for the cell line           |
| Culture vessel          | No visible contamination            |
| Microscopic examination | No evidence of microorganisms       |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Inspect cultures before every medium change or passage.
* Compare cultures with previous observations whenever possible.
* Examine multiple microscopic fields.
* Do not ignore subtle morphological changes.
* Isolate suspected contaminated cultures immediately.

---

## Documentation

Record the following:

* Date
* Cell line
* Passage number
* Culture vessel
* Observations
* Suspected contamination type (if applicable)
* Operator initials

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Routine inspections are performed.
* Observations are documented consistently.
* Suspected cultures are isolated promptly.
* Laboratory records are complete.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                                                  | Possible Cause                    | Corrective Action                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medium becomes cloudy                                        | Possible bacterial contamination  | Isolate the culture and investigate immediately                                 |
| Filamentous structures observed                              | Possible fungal contamination     | Remove the culture from routine use and report the finding                      |
| Small refractile particles showing rapid movement            | Possible bacterial contamination  | Confirm by further examination and discontinue use if contamination is verified |
| Gradual decline in cell growth without obvious contamination | Possible mycoplasma contamination | Perform routine mycoplasma testing                                              |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Handle all cultures as potentially biohazardous.
* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Perform all manipulations in a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet.
* Minimize handling of suspected contaminated cultures.
* Dispose of confirmed contaminated cultures according to institutional biosafety procedures.
