# SOP-012: Operation of a Class II Biosafety Cabinet

## Purpose

To ensure the safe and aseptic operation of a Class II Biosafety Cabinet (BSC) during mammalian cell culture procedures.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all personnel performing cell culture work in a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Verifying the cabinet is functioning properly before use.
* Maintaining aseptic workflow.
* Preventing disruption of cabinet airflow.
* Cleaning and disinfecting the cabinet before and after use.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* 70% ethanol
* Appropriate disinfectant (if required by institutional policy)

### Equipment

* Certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Sterile laboratory consumables
* Biohazard waste container
* Laboratory wipes

---

## Procedure

### 1. Pre-Operation Checks

Confirm that:

* The biosafety cabinet certification is current.
* The airflow alarm is not activated.
* The sash is positioned at the recommended operating height.
* The work surface is clean and free of unnecessary materials.

---

### 2. Prepare the Cabinet

Turn on the cabinet according to laboratory procedures.

Allow the cabinet to operate for the recommended stabilization period before beginning work.

Disinfect the work surface with 70% ethanol.

Arrange materials to maintain an organized workflow.

---

### 3. Perform Cell Culture Procedures

Work well within the interior work area.

Keep materials organized from clean to contaminated.

Avoid blocking the front or rear air grilles.

Perform slow, deliberate movements to minimize airflow disruption.

Minimize unnecessary entry and exit of hands and materials.

---

### 4. Complete the Procedure

Seal all culture vessels.

Remove waste and contaminated materials.

Disinfect all reusable equipment before removal.

Disinfect the work surface thoroughly.

Allow the cabinet to continue operating according to laboratory policy before shutting it down, if applicable.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter            | Acceptance Criterion              |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Airflow              | Unobstructed throughout operation |
| Work surface         | Clean before and after use        |
| Cabinet organization | Minimal clutter                   |
| Cabinet status       | No alarms or operational faults   |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Maintain the sash at the recommended operating height.
* Do not block front or rear air grilles.
* Avoid rapid arm movements inside the cabinet.
* Keep only essential materials inside the work area.
* Separate clean and contaminated materials throughout the procedure.

---

## Documentation

Record the following when required:

* Date
* Operator
* Procedure performed
* Cabinet identification number
* Any equipment malfunction or abnormal observations

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Cabinet certification is current.
* Airflow indicators function normally.
* Cabinet surfaces are properly disinfected.
* Cabinet is free of unnecessary materials.
* Waste has been removed following the procedure.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                      | Possible Cause                            | Corrective Action                               |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Airflow alarm activated          | Obstructed airflow or cabinet malfunction | Stop work and investigate before continuing     |
| Excessive clutter                | Poor workspace organization               | Remove unnecessary materials from the cabinet   |
| Frequent contamination           | Improper aseptic workflow                 | Review cabinet practices and airflow management |
| Difficulty maintaining sterility | Excessive movement inside cabinet         | Work slowly and maintain proper workflow        |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Never use the cabinet if airflow or safety alarms indicate malfunction.
* Do not use open flames inside the biosafety cabinet.
* Dispose of biological waste according to institutional biosafety procedures.
* Report cabinet malfunctions immediately.
