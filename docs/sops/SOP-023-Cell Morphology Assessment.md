# SOP-023: Cell Morphology Assessment

## Purpose

To standardize the routine microscopic assessment of mammalian cell morphology for monitoring culture health, detecting abnormalities, and identifying potential contamination.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to routine morphological assessment of adherent and suspension mammalian cell cultures using an inverted microscope.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Performing routine morphological assessments.
* Recording abnormal observations.
* Identifying cultures that require further investigation.
* Reporting suspected contamination or unexpected morphological changes.

---

## Required Materials

### Equipment

* Inverted microscope
* Cell culture vessels
* Laboratory notebook or electronic record system

---

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare the Culture

Remove the culture from the incubator.

Inspect the culture vessel for:

* Proper labeling
* Medium appearance
* Signs of leakage or damage

---

### 2. Examine Under the Microscope

Observe the culture using an inverted microscope.

Evaluate:

* Overall cell morphology
* Cell attachment (adherent cultures)
* Cell distribution
* Cell density
* Cell aggregation (suspension cultures)

---

### 3. Assess Cell Health

Compare the observed morphology with the expected appearance for the cell line.

Look for:

* Uniform cell shape
* Healthy cytoplasmic appearance
* Normal cell boundaries
* Appropriate cell-to-cell spacing

---

### 4. Identify Abnormal Findings

Record any abnormalities such as:

* Cell rounding
* Cell shrinkage
* Excessive debris
* Vacuolization
* Cell detachment
* Excessive aggregation
* Abnormal morphology
* Possible contamination

---

### 5. Document Findings

Record all observations before returning the culture to the incubator or proceeding with additional procedures.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter         | Acceptance Criterion       |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| Cell morphology   | Typical for the cell line  |
| Cell attachment   | Normal (adherent cultures) |
| Cell distribution | Uniform                    |
| Cell debris       | Minimal                    |
| Contamination     | None observed              |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Evaluate cultures before performing any manipulation.
* Compare observations with previous assessments whenever possible.
* Assess the entire culture rather than isolated fields.
* Record abnormalities immediately.
* If contamination is suspected, isolate the culture until confirmed.

---

## Documentation

Record the following:

* Date
* Cell line
* Passage number
* Culture vessel
* Morphological observations
* Evidence of contamination (if any)
* Operator initials

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Microscope is functioning properly.
* Culture vessel is clearly labeled.
* Cell morphology is consistent with previous observations.
* Records are complete and accurate.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation               | Possible Cause                               | Corrective Action                                                                |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rounded adherent cells    | Cell stress or poor attachment               | Review culture conditions and monitor closely                                    |
| Increased cellular debris | Reduced cell viability                       | Assess cell viability and determine whether passaging or recovery is appropriate |
| Excessive aggregation     | High cell density or inadequate resuspension | Optimize culture density and gently resuspend suspension cultures                |
| Unusual morphology        | Culture stress or contamination              | Investigate culture conditions and evaluate for contamination                    |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Handle all cultures as potentially biohazardous.
* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Minimize the time cultures remain outside the incubator.
* Report suspected contamination immediately.
* Return cultures promptly after examination.
