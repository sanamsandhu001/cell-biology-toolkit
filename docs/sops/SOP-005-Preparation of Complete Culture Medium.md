# SOP-005: Preparation of Complete Culture Medium

## Purpose

To prepare sterile complete culture medium by supplementing basal medium with the appropriate components according to the requirements of the cell line.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to the preparation of complete media for routine mammalian cell culture.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Using sterile technique throughout media preparation.
* Selecting the correct basal medium and supplements.
* Preparing media according to the required formulation.
* Properly labeling and storing prepared media.
* Recording preparation details.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Basal culture medium
* Required supplements (e.g., FBS, GlutaMAX, antibiotics, growth factors)
* Sterile PBS (if required)

### Equipment

* Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Sterile serological pipettes
* Pipette aid
* Micropipettes and sterile tips
* Sterile filter units (if applicable)
* Labels and laboratory marker

---

## Procedure

### 1. Preparation

Bring all components to the recommended working temperature if required by the protocol.

Inspect all reagents for:

* Correct labeling
* Expiry date
* Cloudiness
* Precipitation
* Evidence of contamination

Do not use compromised reagents.

---

### 2. Prepare the Biosafety Cabinet

Disinfect the work surface.

Arrange all materials to minimize unnecessary movement during preparation.

Maintain aseptic technique throughout the procedure.

---

### 3. Add Supplements

Add supplements to the basal medium according to the formulation specified for the cell line.

Mix gently after each addition.

Avoid vigorous shaking to minimize foaming.

---

### 4. Mix the Medium

Invert the bottle gently several times until the supplements are evenly distributed.

Do not vortex.

---

### 5. Label the Medium

Label the container with:

* Medium name
* Supplement composition (optional)
* Preparation date
* Expiry date (according to laboratory policy)
* Operator initials

---

### 6. Storage

Store prepared complete medium under the recommended conditions for the formulation.

Protect light-sensitive media or supplements from prolonged light exposure.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter           | Acceptance Criterion        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Medium appearance   | Clear and free of particles |
| Sterility           | No visible contamination    |
| Labeling            | Complete and legible        |
| Supplement addition | Correct formulation         |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Maintain aseptic technique throughout preparation.
* Verify all reagent identities before use.
* Use only sterile pipettes and tips.
* Add supplements in the correct amounts.
* Avoid repeated warming and cooling of media whenever possible.

---

## Documentation

Record the following:

* Date of preparation
* Medium formulation
* Lot numbers of critical supplements (if required)
* Operator initials
* Expiry date

---

## Quality Control

Before use, verify:

* Medium is clear.
* No precipitation is present.
* No evidence of microbial contamination.
* Correct labeling.
* Proper storage conditions have been maintained.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation           | Possible Cause                               | Corrective Action                                     |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Medium appears cloudy | Possible contamination                       | Discard and prepare fresh medium                      |
| Visible precipitate   | Improper storage or incompatible supplements | Verify reagent compatibility and storage conditions   |
| Incorrect formulation | Supplement omitted or added incorrectly      | Prepare a fresh batch using the correct formulation   |
| Poor cell growth      | Incorrect medium composition                 | Confirm medium formulation for the specific cell line |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Perform all procedures in a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet.
* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Handle all biological reagents according to institutional biosafety guidelines.
* Dispose of waste using approved biohazard procedures.
