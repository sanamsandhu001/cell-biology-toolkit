# Standard Operating Procedures

Welcome to the **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** section of the Cell Biology Toolkit.

This collection provides standardized, step-by-step laboratory protocols designed to support reliable and reproducible mammalian cell culture.

## Topics Included

- Cell thawing
- Cell passaging
- Cryopreservation
- Cell counting and viability
- Media preparation
- Cell seeding
- Aseptic technique
- Biosafety cabinet operation
- Equipment operation
- Waste disposal
- And many more...
