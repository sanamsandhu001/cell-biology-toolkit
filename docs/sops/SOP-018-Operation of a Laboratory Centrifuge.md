# SOP-018: Operation of a Laboratory Centrifuge

## Purpose

To safely and consistently operate a laboratory centrifuge for routine mammalian cell culture applications while protecting samples, personnel, and equipment.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to bench-top centrifuges used for processing mammalian cell suspensions, culture media, and laboratory reagents.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Inspecting the centrifuge before use.
* Balancing all samples correctly.
* Selecting the appropriate operating parameters.
* Reporting equipment damage or malfunction.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Cell suspension or laboratory sample

### Equipment

* Laboratory centrifuge
* Compatible rotor
* Sterile centrifuge tubes
* Tube rack
* Personal protective equipment (PPE)

---

## Procedure

### 1. Pre-Use Inspection

Before use, verify that:

* The centrifuge is clean.
* The rotor is securely installed.
* Tubes are compatible with the selected rotor.
* No visible damage is present.

---

### 2. Prepare Samples

Ensure that:

* Tubes are properly capped.
* Samples are correctly labeled.
* Tube volumes are appropriate for the selected rotor.

---

### 3. Balance the Rotor

Arrange tubes so that opposing positions contain tubes of equal mass.

Do not operate the centrifuge with an unbalanced rotor.

---

### 4. Set Operating Parameters

Select the required:

* Speed
* Time
* Temperature (if applicable)

Verify the settings before starting the run.

---

### 5. Operate the Centrifuge

Close the centrifuge lid securely.

Start the run.

Do not attempt to open the lid while the rotor is in motion.

Allow the rotor to stop completely before opening the centrifuge.

---

### 6. Remove Samples

Carefully remove the tubes without disturbing pellets when present.

Inspect the tubes for leakage or damage before proceeding to the next step.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter     | Acceptance Criterion                         |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Rotor balance | Properly balanced                            |
| Tubes         | Intact with no leakage                       |
| Equipment     | Normal operation without excessive vibration |
| Samples       | Successfully processed                       |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Always balance the rotor before operation.
* Use only compatible tubes and rotors.
* Verify operating parameters before starting.
* Do not exceed the manufacturer's recommended limits.
* Wait until the rotor has completely stopped before opening the lid.

---

## Documentation

Record the following when required:

* Date
* Sample identification
* Centrifuge identification
* Operating parameters
* Operator initials

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Rotor is securely installed.
* Samples remain properly labeled.
* No tube leakage occurred.
* No abnormal vibration or noise was observed.
* Equipment is clean after use.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                  | Possible Cause                                          | Corrective Action                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Excessive vibration          | Unbalanced rotor                                        | Stop the centrifuge immediately and rebalance the tubes                 |
| Loud or unusual noise        | Rotor improperly installed or damaged                   | Inspect the rotor and discontinue use if damage is suspected            |
| Tube leakage                 | Damaged or improperly sealed tubes                      | Replace damaged tubes and repeat the procedure                          |
| Centrifuge lid will not open | Rotor has not completely stopped or safety lock engaged | Wait until the run has ended and follow the manufacturer's instructions |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Never operate the centrifuge with an unbalanced load.
* Do not open the lid until the rotor has completely stopped.
* Inspect tubes for cracks before use.
* Clean spills immediately according to laboratory safety procedures.
