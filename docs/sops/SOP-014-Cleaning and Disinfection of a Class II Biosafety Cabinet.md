# SOP-014: Cleaning and Disinfection of a Class II Biosafety Cabinet

## Purpose

To ensure that the Class II Biosafety Cabinet (BSC) remains clean, sterile, and suitable for mammalian cell culture by following standardized cleaning and disinfection procedures.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to the routine cleaning and disinfection of Class II Biosafety Cabinets used for mammalian cell culture before and after laboratory procedures.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Cleaning the biosafety cabinet before and after each use.
* Using appropriate disinfectants.
* Removing biological waste and spills promptly.
* Reporting cabinet damage or malfunction.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* 70% ethanol
* Laboratory-approved disinfectant (if required)

### Equipment

* Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Lint-free wipes or sterile disposable wipes
* Biohazard waste container

---

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare the Cabinet

Ensure that all cultures, reagents, and waste have been removed from the cabinet.

Inspect the work surface for spills or debris.

---

### 2. Clean Interior Surfaces

Using lint-free wipes moistened with 70% ethanol or an approved disinfectant, clean the following surfaces:

* Work surface
* Side walls
* Back wall
* Interior glass sash (accessible portion)

Clean from the cleanest area toward the dirtiest area.

---

### 3. Remove Residues

If spills or dried residues are present, remove them carefully before final disinfection.

Avoid using abrasive materials that may damage cabinet surfaces.

---

### 4. Disinfect Equipment

Disinfect reusable laboratory equipment before removing it from the cabinet.

Discard disposable items into the appropriate biohazard waste container.

---

### 5. Final Disinfection

Perform a final wipe of the work surface and interior surfaces.

Allow the disinfectant to remain on the surfaces for the recommended contact time.

---

### 6. Complete the Procedure

Leave the cabinet clean and free of unnecessary materials.

Return the cabinet to standby or operating mode according to laboratory policy.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter         | Acceptance Criterion              |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Work surface      | Clean and free of visible residue |
| Interior surfaces | Properly disinfected              |
| Waste             | Removed from cabinet              |
| Cabinet condition | Ready for the next user           |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Perform cleaning before and after each cell culture session.
* Observe the recommended disinfectant contact time.
* Avoid excessive liquid that could enter cabinet openings.
* Never block airflow grilles during cleaning.
* Use only laboratory-approved cleaning agents.

---

## Documentation

Record the following when required:

* Date
* Cabinet identification
* Cleaning completed
* Operator initials
* Any abnormalities or maintenance needs

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Interior surfaces are visibly clean.
* No biological waste remains inside the cabinet.
* Cabinet airflow openings are unobstructed.
* Cleaning supplies are suitable for laboratory use.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                   | Possible Cause                       | Corrective Action                                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Persistent stains             | Dried biological material            | Clean with an appropriate laboratory-approved disinfectant and repeat if necessary |
| Sticky residue                | Disinfectant buildup                 | Wipe with sterile water if recommended by laboratory procedures                    |
| Visible debris after cleaning | Incomplete cleaning                  | Repeat cleaning before using the cabinet                                           |
| Cabinet damage observed       | Mechanical wear or accidental damage | Report the issue and discontinue use if cabinet integrity is compromised           |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Handle all waste as potentially biohazardous.
* Use only disinfectants approved by institutional policy.
* Dispose of contaminated cleaning materials as biohazardous waste.
* Report spills involving hazardous materials according to laboratory procedures.
