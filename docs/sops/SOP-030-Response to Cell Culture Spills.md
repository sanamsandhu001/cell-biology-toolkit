# SOP-030: Response to Cell Culture Spills

## Purpose

To provide a standardized procedure for safely containing, disinfecting, and reporting spills involving mammalian cell cultures, culture media, and associated biological materials.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to accidental spills occurring inside or outside a Class II Biosafety Cabinet during mammalian cell culture procedures.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Responding to spills promptly and safely.
* Preventing the spread of contamination.
* Using appropriate disinfectants.
* Reporting significant spills or exposures according to institutional policy.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Laboratory-approved disinfectant
* 70% ethanol (for final surface cleaning, if applicable)

### Equipment

* Absorbent laboratory wipes or paper towels
* Biohazard waste container
* Forceps or tongs (if required)
* Personal protective equipment (PPE)

---

## Procedure

### 1. Secure the Area

Stop work immediately.

Restrict unnecessary movement around the spill to prevent contamination.

If the spill occurs inside a biosafety cabinet, keep the cabinet operating throughout the cleanup.

---

### 2. Wear Appropriate PPE

Before beginning cleanup, ensure appropriate PPE is worn.

Replace contaminated gloves if necessary.

---
