# SOP-032: Establishment and Management of Master and Working Cell Banks

## Purpose

To establish standardized procedures for creating, maintaining, and using Master Cell Banks (MCBs) and Working Cell Banks (WCBs) to preserve cell line integrity and ensure experimental reproducibility.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all mammalian cell lines maintained for long-term laboratory use.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Establishing Master and Working Cell Banks.
* Ensuring cultures meet laboratory quality requirements before banking.
* Maintaining accurate inventory records.
* Preventing unnecessary passage of Master Cell Bank stocks.

---

## Definitions

### Master Cell Bank (MCB)

A collection of cryopreserved vials prepared from a single authenticated, contamination-free cell culture that serves as the primary long-term reference stock.

### Working Cell Bank (WCB)

A collection of cryopreserved vials prepared from the Master Cell Bank and used for routine laboratory work.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Cryopreservation medium
* Complete culture medium

### Equipment

* Cryovials
* Controlled-rate freezing container (if used)
* Liquid nitrogen storage system
* Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Laboratory inventory records

---

## Procedure

### 1. Verify Culture Quality

Before banking, confirm that the culture:

* Exhibits normal morphology.
* Is free from contamination.
* Meets laboratory quality requirements.

---

### 2. Establish the Master Cell Bank

Prepare multiple cryovials from a healthy, low-passage culture.

Assign each vial a unique identifier.

Store all MCB vials in designated long-term cryostorage.

---

### 3. Establish the Working Cell Bank

Recover a vial from the MCB.

Expand the culture under routine conditions.

Prepare multiple cryovials to create the WCB.

Store WCB vials separately from the MCB.

---

### 4. Routine Laboratory Use

Use only WCB vials for routine experiments whenever possible.

Reserve MCB vials for establishing new WCBs or replacing exhausted stocks.

---

### 5. Inventory Management

Maintain an inventory including:

* Cell line name
* Bank type (MCB or WCB)
* Cryovial identification
* Storage location
* Date of preparation
* Passage number

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter          | Acceptance Criterion      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Cell morphology    | Typical for the cell line |
| Contamination      | None detected             |
| Cell bank labeling | Complete and accurate     |
| Inventory          | Current and traceable     |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Establish the MCB from low-passage cultures.
* Authenticate the cell line before banking.
* Confirm negative mycoplasma status before cryopreservation.
* Never use MCB stocks for routine experiments.
* Maintain separate storage records for MCB and WCB.

---

## Documentation

Record the following:

* Cell line
* Bank type (MCB/WCB)
* Passage number
* Number of cryovials
* Storage location
* Date of banking
* Operator initials

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Cell identity has been authenticated.
* Mycoplasma testing has been completed.
* Cryovials are correctly labeled.
* Inventory records are current.
* Storage locations are accurate.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                | Possible Cause              | Corrective Action                                               |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low recovery after thawing | Poor-quality source culture | Establish a new bank from a healthy culture                     |
| Mislabeled cryovials       | Documentation error         | Verify inventory and relabel according to laboratory policy     |
| MCB depleted               | Excessive routine use       | Establish a new MCB from an authenticated early-passage culture |
| Inventory discrepancies    | Incomplete record keeping   | Audit storage records and update inventory                      |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Handle cryogenic materials using appropriate PPE.
* Maintain aseptic technique during all banking procedures.
* Treat all cultures as potentially biohazardous.
* Dispose of defective cryovials according to laboratory biosafety procedures.
