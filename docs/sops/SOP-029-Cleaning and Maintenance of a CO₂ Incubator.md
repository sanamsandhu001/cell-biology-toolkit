# SOP-029: Cleaning and Maintenance of a CO₂ Incubator

## Purpose

To ensure routine cleaning and maintenance of a CO₂ incubator to minimize contamination and maintain optimal culture conditions.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all CO₂ incubators used for mammalian cell culture.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Performing routine cleaning according to laboratory schedules.
* Maintaining incubator hygiene.
* Reporting equipment malfunction or contamination.
* Documenting maintenance activities.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Laboratory-approved disinfectant
* 70% ethanol
* Sterile distilled or deionized water

### Equipment

* Laboratory wipes
* Personal protective equipment (PPE)
* Biohazard waste container

---

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare the Incubator

Remove all cell cultures, media, and accessories.

Switch the incubator to maintenance mode or power off if required by laboratory procedures.

---

### 2. Remove Internal Components

Remove:

* Shelves
* Shelf supports
* Water reservoir
* Removable trays

Inspect all components for visible contamination.

---

### 3. Clean Interior Surfaces

Wipe all accessible interior surfaces using a laboratory-approved disinfectant.

After the recommended contact time, remove any disinfectant residue if required.

---

### 4. Clean Removable Components

Clean removable shelves and accessories individually.

Allow them to dry completely before reinstallation.

---

### 5. Replace Water Reservoir

Discard remaining water.

Clean the reservoir thoroughly.

Refill with sterile distilled or deionized water according to laboratory policy.

---

### 6. Reassemble the Incubator

Reinstall all components.

Verify that shelves are correctly positioned.

Restart the incubator if applicable.

Allow environmental conditions to stabilize before returning cultures.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter               | Acceptance Criterion              |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Interior surfaces       | Clean and free of visible residue |
| Shelves and accessories | Clean and dry                     |
| Water reservoir         | Clean and freshly filled          |
| Incubator operation     | Normal after maintenance          |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Remove all cultures before cleaning.
* Use only laboratory-approved cleaning agents.
* Replace the humidity water regularly.
* Allow the incubator to stabilize before use.
* Record all maintenance activities.

---

## Documentation

Record the following:

* Date
* Incubator identification
* Cleaning performed
* Water reservoir replaced
* Operator initials
* Maintenance observations

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Interior surfaces are clean.
* Shelves are correctly installed.
* Water reservoir contains fresh sterile water.
* Temperature and CO₂ return to normal operating conditions.
* Maintenance log has been completed.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                    | Possible Cause                     | Corrective Action                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Persistent contamination       | Incomplete cleaning                | Repeat cleaning and inspect all removable components     |
| Mineral deposits               | Infrequent reservoir cleaning      | Clean thoroughly and refill with sterile distilled water |
| Temperature or CO₂ instability | Insufficient stabilization time    | Allow the incubator to equilibrate before use            |
| Unpleasant odor                | Microbial growth or stagnant water | Perform complete cleaning and replace the humidity water |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Handle cleaning agents according to institutional safety guidelines.
* Disconnect electrical power if required before maintenance.
* Dispose of contaminated water and cleaning materials appropriately.
* Report equipment malfunction immediately.
