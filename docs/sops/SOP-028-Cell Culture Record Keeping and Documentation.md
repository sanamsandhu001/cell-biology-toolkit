# SOP-028: Cell Culture Record Keeping and Documentation

## Purpose

To standardize the documentation of mammalian cell culture activities, ensuring traceability, reproducibility, and accurate laboratory records.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all mammalian cell culture procedures performed within the laboratory, including routine maintenance, experimental work, cryopreservation, and quality control.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Recording all cell culture activities promptly.
* Maintaining accurate and complete records.
* Correcting documentation errors according to laboratory policy.
* Ensuring records remain legible and traceable.

---

## Required Materials

### Equipment

* Laboratory notebook or electronic laboratory notebook (ELN)
* Cell culture record sheets
* Permanent laboratory marker
* Labels

---

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the Culture

Verify and record:

* Cell line name
* Culture identification number (if applicable)
* Passage number
* Culture vessel

---

### 2. Record Routine Procedures

Document all routine activities, including:

* Medium changes
* Passaging
* Cell seeding
* Cryopreservation
* Cell recovery
* Cell counting
* Quality control procedures

---

### 3. Record Experimental Information

When applicable, document:

* Experimental purpose
* Treatment conditions
* Reagents used
* Sample identifiers
* Collection dates

---

### 4. Record Observations

Document any relevant observations, including:

* Cell morphology
* Confluency
* Viability
* Growth characteristics
* Abnormal findings
* Suspected contamination

---

### 5. Review Documentation

Review entries for completeness before concluding the procedure.

Correct any documentation errors according to laboratory policy without obscuring the original entry.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter                | Acceptance Criterion  |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Cell line identification | Complete              |
| Passage number           | Recorded              |
| Procedure details        | Complete              |
| Documentation            | Legible and traceable |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Record information immediately after completing each procedure.
* Maintain consistent cell line naming.
* Use permanent ink for paper records.
* Never erase or obscure previous entries.
* Maintain chronological records.

---

## Documentation

Each record should include:

* Date
* Cell line
* Passage number
* Procedure performed
* Culture vessel
* Operator initials
* Observations
* Deviations (if applicable)

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* All entries are complete.
* Cell line identification is accurate.
* Passage numbers are updated correctly.
* Documentation is signed or initialed.
* Records are stored securely.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                  | Possible Cause           | Corrective Action                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Missing information          | Incomplete documentation | Complete records immediately while information is available |
| Incorrect passage number     | Recording error          | Correct according to laboratory documentation policy        |
| Inconsistent cell line names | Non-standard naming      | Use standardized laboratory nomenclature                    |
| Untraceable samples          | Inadequate labeling      | Verify sample labels before every procedure                 |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Protect laboratory records from damage or unauthorized modification.
* Maintain confidentiality of research records where applicable.
* Ensure electronic records are regularly backed up according to institutional policy.
