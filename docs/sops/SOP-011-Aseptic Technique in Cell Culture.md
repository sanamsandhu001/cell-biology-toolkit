# SOP-011: Aseptic Technique in Cell Culture

## Purpose

To minimize the risk of microbial contamination during mammalian cell culture by implementing standardized aseptic handling procedures.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all routine mammalian cell culture procedures performed within a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Following aseptic technique throughout all procedures.
* Maintaining a clean working environment.
* Preventing cross-contamination between cultures.
* Reporting suspected contamination immediately.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* 70% ethanol
* Sterile culture media and reagents

### Equipment

* Class II Biosafety Cabinet
* Sterile pipettes and pipette tips
* Sterile culture vessels
* Biohazard waste container

---

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare the Work Area

Disinfect the biosafety cabinet work surface with 70% ethanol before beginning work.

Arrange only the materials required for the procedure inside the cabinet.

Allow sufficient space to maintain an organized workflow.

---

### 2. Prepare Materials

Inspect all media, reagents, and culture vessels before use.

Disinfect the exterior surfaces of containers before placing them inside the cabinet.

Keep sterile items sealed until required.

---

### 3. Perform Cell Culture Procedures

Work slowly and deliberately.

Open culture vessels only when necessary.

Keep lids, caps, and containers exposed for the shortest possible time.

Avoid placing hands or equipment over open sterile containers.

Use sterile pipettes or tips for every transfer.

Avoid reusing disposable sterile items.

---

### 4. Prevent Cross-Contamination

Handle only one cell line at a time whenever possible.

Change pipettes and tips between samples.

Keep culture vessels clearly labeled throughout the procedure.

---

### 5. Complete the Procedure

Close all culture vessels securely.

Remove waste promptly.

Disinfect the work surface after completing the procedure.

Return materials to their designated storage locations.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter         | Acceptance Criterion                |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Work surface      | Clean and disinfected               |
| Culture vessels   | Properly closed and labeled         |
| Sterile materials | Maintained under aseptic conditions |
| Cell cultures     | No evidence of contamination        |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Perform all work inside a certified biosafety cabinet.
* Minimize unnecessary movement during procedures.
* Avoid blocking airflow within the cabinet.
* Never reuse sterile disposable items.
* Keep exposure of sterile materials to a minimum.

---

## Documentation

Record the following when applicable:

* Date
* Procedure performed
* Cell line
* Operator initials
* Any contamination or deviations observed

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Biosafety cabinet is clean before and after use.
* Sterile consumables remain unopened until needed.
* Culture vessels remain properly labeled.
* No contamination is detected during routine culture monitoring.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                                    | Possible Cause         | Corrective Action                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recurrent contamination                        | Poor aseptic technique | Review workflow and reinforce aseptic practices                         |
| Contamination in multiple cultures             | Cross-contamination    | Handle one cell line at a time and replace consumables between cultures |
| Frequent contamination after media preparation | Non-sterile handling   | Verify sterility of reagents and preparation procedures                 |
| Cluttered work area                            | Poor organization      | Arrange materials before beginning work                                 |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Treat all cell cultures as potentially biohazardous.
* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Perform all procedures in a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet.
* Dispose of biohazardous waste according to institutional regulations.
* Wash hands before and after completing cell culture work.
