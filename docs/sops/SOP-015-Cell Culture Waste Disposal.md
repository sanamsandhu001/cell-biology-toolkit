# SOP-015: Cell Culture Waste Disposal

## Purpose

To ensure the safe handling, segregation, decontamination, and disposal of waste generated during mammalian cell culture procedures while minimizing risks to personnel and the environment.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all biological and laboratory waste generated during routine mammalian cell culture work.

---

## Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

* Segregating waste according to laboratory policy.
* Disposing of waste in designated containers.
* Preventing contamination during waste handling.
* Reporting spills or accidental exposures immediately.

---

## Required Materials

### Reagents

* Laboratory-approved disinfectant
* 70% ethanol (if required)

### Equipment

* Biohazard waste bags
* Sharps container
* Liquid waste container
* Laboratory wipes
* Personal protective equipment (PPE)

---

## Procedure

### 1. Segregate Waste

Separate waste into the appropriate categories:

* Biological waste
* Liquid waste
* Sharps
* Non-contaminated laboratory waste

Do not mix incompatible waste streams.

---

### 2. Dispose of Solid Biological Waste

Place contaminated disposable items, including:

* Pipette tips
* Serological pipettes
* Gloves
* Culture plates
* Culture flasks
* Tubes

into designated biohazard waste containers.

Do not overfill waste bags or containers.

---

### 3. Dispose of Liquid Waste

Collect liquid biological waste in designated containers.

Treat or dispose of liquid waste according to institutional biosafety procedures before final disposal.

---

### 4. Dispose of Sharps

Immediately discard needles, blades, broken glass, and other sharps into approved sharps containers.

Do not recap, bend, or manipulate sharps prior to disposal.

---

### 5. Clean the Work Area

After waste removal:

* Remove any visible contamination.
* Disinfect the work surface.
* Dispose of cleaning materials as appropriate.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter            | Acceptance Criterion                     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Waste segregation    | Correct waste container used             |
| Biohazard containers | Properly sealed and not overfilled       |
| Work area            | Clean and disinfected                    |
| Sharps disposal      | All sharps placed in approved containers |

---

## Critical Control Points

* Segregate waste immediately after use.
* Never compress biohazard waste bags by hand.
* Dispose of sharps immediately after use.
* Prevent leakage from liquid waste containers.
* Follow institutional biosafety and environmental regulations.

---

## Documentation

Record the following when required:

* Date
* Waste disposal activity
* Operator initials
* Spill or exposure incidents
* Deviations from standard procedures

---

## Quality Control

Verify:

* Waste containers are correctly labeled.
* Biohazard bags are not overfilled.
* Sharps containers remain below the maximum fill level.
* Liquid waste containers are securely closed.
* Work surfaces are disinfected after completion.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Observation                           | Possible Cause              | Corrective Action                                                          |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overflowing waste container           | Delayed disposal            | Replace the container before it reaches capacity                           |
| Mixed waste types                     | Improper segregation        | Re-educate personnel and follow institutional waste segregation procedures |
| Liquid waste leakage                  | Improperly sealed container | Transfer to a secure container and disinfect affected surfaces             |
| Sharps found outside sharps container | Improper disposal           | Safely dispose of sharps immediately and review disposal practices         |

---

## Safety Considerations

* Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Treat all biological waste as potentially infectious.
* Handle sharps with extreme care.
* Clean and disinfect spills immediately according to laboratory procedures.
* Report accidental exposures or injuries to the appropriate supervisor.
